    Questions before you order.

    What do I need to upload?

    Clear, high-resolution photos (JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB each). You can also upload a picture that shows the colour you like.

    How much do I pay now?

    You choose: pay 100% now, or pay 60% now and the remaining 40% before printing. The exact amounts show before you pay.

    How do I see my design?

    After you order you get a private link. Your design proofs appear there as protected previews. You can approve a design or request changes with a comment.

    Can I order a different quantity?

    Yes. Choose Custom quantity and we will send you a quotation, with discounts, on your order page.

    When do I get my final files?

    After full payment, from your order page.
